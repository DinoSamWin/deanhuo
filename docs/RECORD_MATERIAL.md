## Exact generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: a single square 2x2 material texture atlas for compositing over music album artwork in a website, 1536x1536.
Create four DIFFERENT ultra-photorealistic macro scans of lightly used clear polyethylene record sleeve plastic, one per exact quadrant, no gutters, no labels. Each quadrant fills its square perfectly, shot orthographically straight on, on a perfectly BLACK background for SCREEN blend compositing. Only the white and grey reflected-light wrinkles and minute dust are visible; most pixels near black. This is raw texture, NOT an album mockup or a bag silhouette.
Each quadrant must have distinct organically irregular distribution: upper left sparse crumpled corner highlights and short bent crease clusters on left edge; upper right loose fine tangled wrinkles and small cloudy rub marks on lower right edge; lower left aged film with soft irregular crinkles and a few thin wavy folds entering from top; lower right barely wrinkled sleeve with localized irregular puckering and rubbed micro-scratches on right side.
Use realistic asymmetric wrinkles, variable-width and variable-brightness reflections, creases bending and branching unpredictably, little trapped dust, wispy scuffs, slightly cloudy finger rubs. Edge wear and corner compression, centers mostly clear so underlying album artwork stays legible. Subdued fine-grained analog material, natural aged plastic, realistic photographic microdetail. No regular diagonal light bars, no repeated designs, no parallel straight glossy stripes, no perfect rectangular border, no text, no watermark, no objects, no albums, no colored artwork, no borders between quadrants.
